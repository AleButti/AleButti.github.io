About me

Theoretical & Computational Neuroscience

I am a first-year PhD student in Statistics and Computer Science at Bocconi University, with a background in Biomedical Engineering at Politecnico di Milano.

My interests lie in theoretical and computational neuroscience. I am particularly interested in mathematical models of neural circuits and in understanding how connectivity and interactions between neurons give rise to collective dynamics and computation. More broadly, I am interested in the mathematical modeling of complex biological systems.

Research

My master’s thesis focused on connectome-informed models of cortical network dynamics, combining connectivity and functional imaging data from the MICrONS dataset with spiking-network simulations.

We are currently preparing a manuscript based on this work with Alessandro Sanzeni and Alberto Antonietti.

Research interests

  • Theoretical and computational neuroscience
  • Neural circuit dynamics and computation
  • Mathematical modeling of complex biological systems
